# Meeting record rules

## Decision

Settled in the notes: an explicit marker ("decided", "agreed", "we go with", "final") or a
choice stated by the person with authority with no recorded objection. A proposal with "maybe",
"we could", "let's think" is not a decision. Record: the decision, the reason if stated ("not
stated" otherwise), who decided, the evidence line.

## Action

A commitment by a named person ("I will", "X said they would") or an assignment the person
accepted. "Someone should", "we need to" are open questions until a name is attached. Record:
the action as a verb phrase, owner, due date (text as given: "Friday", "this week"; `no date`
otherwise), evidence line.

## Open questions

Everything raised and not settled, plus every missing owner or date. Phrase each as a question
with the person who can answer it when obvious.

## Discussed only

Topics that consumed time without decision or action. They go in the recap so nobody assumes
a decision was made.

## Purpose check

If a purpose is given, the recap's first line says whether it was decided. If not given, the
first line is the most consequential decision.

## Evidence

Quote the line, with the speaker when known. Trim only at the ends. The record must be
checkable against the notes.

## Recap formats

| Style | Shape |
|---|---|
| slack | under 120 words; lines starting with "Decided:", a line per action "Name: action by date", "Open:", "Discussed, not decided:" |
| email | under 200 words; first line is the main decision; then actions as a short list; no greeting fluff |
| doc | four sections: Decisions, Actions (table: action, owner, due), Open questions, Discussed |

No praise, no "thanks everyone", no summary of the summary.
