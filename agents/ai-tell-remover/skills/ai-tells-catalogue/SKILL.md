# AI tells catalogue

| Tell | How to spot | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| reveal | "it's not X, it's Y", "this isn't about X, it's about Y", "more than X: Y" | keep Y as a plain statement; delete X |
| negated list | "no X, no Y, no Z" | one item, or a positive statement |
| triad | three parallel adjectives, verbs or clauses for rhythm | keep one or two; drop the weakest |
| em dash glue | dashes joining clauses that could be sentences | full stop, or a comma if truly parenthetical |
| empty opener | "in today's fast-paced world", "let's dive in", "picture this", "I hope this finds you well", "great question" | delete |
| closer | last sentence restating the paragraph, "in conclusion", "ultimately", "at the end of the day" | delete |
| hedge stack | "could potentially", "may possibly", "it's worth noting that", "it's important to remember" | one plain statement, or one explicit uncertainty with a reason |
| uniform cadence | every sentence 15 to 22 words, same shape | split one, merge two, let one be three words |
| trailing clause | sentence ends with ", which makes it...", ", ensuring that...", ", allowing you to..." | stop at the main clause; make the rest its own sentence or cut |
| adjective before every noun | "robust", "seamless", "comprehensive", "powerful", "cutting-edge" | cut the adjective; keep the noun |
| vocabulary | delve, leverage, streamline, unlock, elevate, empower, navigate (a topic), tapestry, landscape, realm, testament, crucial, pivotal, game-changer, journey | plain verb or noun |
| transition tics | "moreover", "furthermore", "additionally", "that said", "on the flip side" | delete or "and", "but" |
| exclamation and emoji | in professional text | delete |
| rhetorical question run | two or more questions in a row | one statement |
| mirrored structure | paragraph two shaped exactly like paragraph one | change one of them |
| summary bullets of a short text | three bullets restating two sentences | prose |

## What is not a tell

Short sentences. Fragments in casual channels. A single list when there are really several
items. A dash in a date range. Technical terms. Numbers.

## Order of edits

1. Delete openers and closers.
2. Cut reveals and negated lists.
3. Break triads and dashes.
4. Fix cadence last, once the content is settled.

## Preserving meaning

Keep every fact, number, name and commitment. If a cut would lose information, keep the
information in plainer words. Never add an example the author did not give.
