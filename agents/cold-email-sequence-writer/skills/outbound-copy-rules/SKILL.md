# Outbound copy rules

## Structure of email 1

1. Trigger line: a fact about them (the event, the role, the season). No greeting fluff.
2. Promise line: the result in their units, under twelve words.
3. Mechanism or proof: one sentence, concrete.
4. Ask: one yes/no question.
5. Sign-off: first name, optionally one word of role.

## Follow-ups

- Each one adds a new reason, never "just following up" or "bumping this".
- Order: mechanism, then proof or offer, then permission to close.
- Reply in the same thread for email follow-ups; the LinkedIn note stands alone.

## Timing

Email 1 day 0, LinkedIn note day 1, follow-up day 3, follow-up day 8, close day 15. Send on
Tuesday to Thursday mornings in the prospect's time zone. Stop on any reply.

## Length

Email body under 80 words, subject under five words, LinkedIn note under 40 words. If the sender's
offer needs more, it is not ready for cold outreach; say so.

## Banned

Phrases: "I hope this finds you well", "my name is", "I'm reaching out", "quick question", "just
checking in", "touching base", "circle back", "game-changer", "leverage", "streamline", "unlock",
"seamless", "cutting-edge", "I'd love to", "no pressure".

Patterns: "it's not X, it's Y"; "no X, no Y, no Z"; three parallel items for rhythm; em dashes;
rhetorical questions in a row; exclamation marks; a paragraph that restates the previous one;
sentences that trail into extra comma clauses.

Tells of generation: perfectly balanced sentence lengths, every email the same shape, adjectives
before every noun. Vary length; let one line be three words.

## Personalisation

Mark with square brackets. Minimum: first name and one trigger detail. Better: one number from
their business. A sequence with no slots is a template, and templates get ignored.

## Proof

Use only what the sender provided. Prefer one specific number over three vague claims. Anonymised
proof ("a two-site group in Lyon") beats invented names. If the sender has no proof, use the
mechanism and say in `questions` what proof would help.

## Compliance

B2B only, one-click opt-out line on email 1 for the EU, no tracking pixels mentioned, no
pretending to know someone they do not know.
