# Profile opportunity method

## What counts as evidence

Strong: outcomes with numbers, named scope (team of 6, 2M EUR budget), a public body of work
(posts, talks, open source), recommendations quoting a specific result, repeated themes across
roles. Weak: a skill in the skills list with nothing in experience backing it, a title alone, a
course certificate, adjectives in the About section.

Count evidence per claim. Two strong items make a claim; one strong plus two weak make a maybe.

## The six kinds

| Kind | The opening looks like | Typical next step |
|---|---|---|
| role | a job title in a segment the experience already serves | rewrite one entry with an outcome, message two people who hire for it |
| client | a customer type the past work solved a problem for | a short case note from a past role, offered to three similar companies |
| partnership | a complementary business the person has worked next to | a message proposing one joint piece or referral |
| content | a theme the posts already touch that the target reader cares about | a series of three posts, one per week |
| community | a group where the target readers gather | join, answer two questions, then post |
| product | a tool or template built repeatedly in past roles | package it once, offer it free to the segment |

## Scoring

Fit: how many pieces of evidence support the claim (0 to 3). Reachability: can the next step be
done this month with what exists (3), after one profile fix (2), after new work (1). Rank by fit
times reachability. Ties: prefer the kind the person has already acted on (posts, community).

## Common gaps and the fix

- No numbers anywhere: pick the one role with a measurable outcome and add it.
- Title inflation or deflation: align the headline with what the target reader calls the job.
- Posts off-topic for the goal: pin one on-topic post, plan three more.
- Skills list of twenty items: cut to eight that match the goal.
- Gap in dates: add one line explaining it; silence reads worse.
- No recommendations: ask two people for a sentence about one specific result.

## Edits with leverage, in order

1. Headline (segment + credibility + what you want to be contacted for).
2. First two lines of About (the reader decides there).
3. The most recent experience entry (one outcome, one scope number).
4. Pinned post.
5. Skills (cut, then order).
